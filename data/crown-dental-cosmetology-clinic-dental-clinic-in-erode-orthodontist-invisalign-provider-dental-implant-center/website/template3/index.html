Common Queries

Frequently Asked

Questions

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your first visit includes a comprehensive consultation, digital X-rays if needed, a review of your medical history, and a discussion of your health goals followed by a preliminary examination.

Do you accept insurance?

Yes, we work with most major insurance providers. Our billing team will help you verify your coverage and maximize your benefits for any procedures.

How do I schedule or cancel an appointment?

You can easily schedule by calling our front desk or using the 'Book Now' button on this site. We ask for a 24-hour notice for any cancellations out of respect for our specialists.

Do you provide Invisalign clear aligners?

Yes—our Invisalign® service lets you straighten teeth discreetly with nearly invisible, removable trays, guided by our certified orthodontics experts.

Have a different question?

Our front-desk team is always available to help you with your queries.
