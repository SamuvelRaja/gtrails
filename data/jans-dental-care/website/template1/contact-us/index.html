Your First Visit

What to Expect

When You Arrive.

We want your visit to be seamless from the moment you walk through our door.

01
Warm Welcome

Our reception team will greet you, help you complete any paperwork, and offer you a comfortable seat in our modern waiting area.

02
Consultation

Your dentist will review your medical history, discuss your concerns, and perform a thorough oral examination including digital X-rays if needed.

03
Treatment Plan

You will receive a clear, honest explanation of your dental health status and a customised treatment plan with transparent pricing.

04
Begin Treatment

If you are ready, we can often begin treatment the same day. Otherwise, we will schedule follow-ups at times that work best for your routine.
